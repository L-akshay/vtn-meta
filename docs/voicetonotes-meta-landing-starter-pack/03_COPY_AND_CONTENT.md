## How it works

### 1. Tap record.
Start a note and speak naturally.

### 2. Keep talking.
Capture ideas, lectures and conversations without stopping to type.

If the production app currently supports audio/video import, this step may instead read:
> Talk, or import an audio or video file.

Do not advertise import unless the exact flow has been verified in the current app.

### 3. Get the note.
Turn the transcript into a cleaner summary, structure and action items.
